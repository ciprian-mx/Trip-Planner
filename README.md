## ✈️ The Idea

Planning a trip with friends usually starts with excitement and quickly turns into chaos: endless group chats, forgotten bookings, and the classic *"wait, who was supposed to buy the train tickets?"*

**Trip Planner** fixes that. Whether it's a **city break in Rome**, a **hiking trip in the Bucegi Mountains**, or a **summer camp** with a dozen people, Trip Planner gives every journey its own home. There you can organize tasks, share ideas, and track progress all the way to departure day.

No more lost messages. No more last-minute surprises. Just a clear plan everyone can follow.

---

## 🌍 What You Can Do

### 🗺️ Create Your Trip
Start a new adventure in seconds. Give it a name, a destination, a short description and your travel dates. Every trip gets its own dedicated page, a single place for everything related to it.

### 👥 Bring Your Crew
Invite friends to join your trip and manage who's coming along. Everyone can be part of several trips at once, from a weekend getaway to the big summer holiday.

### ✅ Organize Activities
Break the trip down into activities: *booking the accommodation*, *buying the train tickets*, *visiting the Art Museum*. Each activity has its own timeline and can include photos or videos, like a picture of the place or a short clip of what's waiting for you.

### 🙋 Share the Work
Assign activities to participants so everyone knows exactly what they're responsible for. Track each one as it moves from **To Do** → **In Progress** → **Done**.

### 💬 Talk It Through
Discuss every activity right where it lives. Leave comments, share suggestions, and keep the conversation organized, in order and in context.

### 📊 Your Personal Dashboard
See everything that matters to you at a glance: your upcoming trips, the activities you're involved in, and what's due soon. Filter by status to focus on what's still in progress or celebrate what's already done.

### 🤖 Your AI Travel Assistant
Every trip comes with a smart assistant that writes an **itinerary summary** for you. It shows the plan day by day, what's already done and what still needs attention:

> *"There are 2 unfinished activities left: booking the accommodation and buying the tickets."*

Ask for a fresh summary anytime to see where your preparations stand.
